Use the same frozen record after an actual delay.

Initial authoring was authorized 33h 04m–33h 06m after the record freeze. Twelve fresh item contexts received the same external record or no record. This was later than the 86400-second minimum, not a trial at exactly 24 hours.

These are recorded actual outcomes, not fresh AI regeneration or a test of one subject’s psychological retention.

Read delayed correctness and optional inspection separately.

On the same six delayed items, acceptance was 6/6 without the record → 6/6 with the same frozen record. Optional schema inspections were 6 → 6.

Paired items show 0 corrected and 0 regressed. Six items per arm are three further objectives × two formats, not six independent objectives or experiences.

Acceptance was equal; this sample shows no accuracy gain.

The predeclared equal-acceptance/fewer-inspections/no-added-errors criterion was not met. These results do not establish an accuracy or optional-inspection benefit from the record.

Optional-inspection cost is not total source I/O. Every action executor reads its assigned source to compute an artifact in both arms. Speed, token, compute and spending savings were not measured.

Actual elapsed time, separate from scheduling.

Initial/final authoring clocks are real pre-call authoring authorization receipts. Provider sampling starts are unavailable and are not inferred. Execution clocks are actual native item started_at timestamps. Inspection work is recorded separately from authoring authorization.

The three stage-record local modification times are original state-file filesystem mtimes, not instrumented exact completion timestamps. All three raw phase-entry authorization guards are separately shown and are not completion times. A phase-entry may follow the last output; it is not relabeled as a provider or completion timestamp. This is local clock evidence, not an external signature or attestation. Original trial records and their times were not altered.

Read this as reuse of an external record.

The comparison is access to the whole frozen external record within the delayed block. Objectives and time both differ from the immediate trial, so immediate-to-delayed differences are not a time-only effect or forgetting. Fresh contexts rereading an external record do not establish one subject’s psychological retention, weight learning, natural conversation memory or a forgetting curve.

The training review template explicitly requested legacy_scope; this is not unprompted conceptual learning.

These are engineered file tasks over known public numeric sources; prior exposure to previously public values cannot be excluded. Roles share a host with read allowlists rather than OS isolation. Provider/model/settings/token/compute metadata is unverified and null. Private native LRE, SHIORI mechanisms/speed, native Wake, Emotion, biological brains, AGI and consciousness were not tested.

The 2026-10-28 immediate publication keeps its at-publication pending state unchanged. These actually collected results append a separate date, data file and replay. All ten earlier studies and four overview animations are preserved.

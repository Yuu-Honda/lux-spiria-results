STUDY / RETRIEVAL HYPOTHESES / v1

More relevant hits.
Mix-ups remained.

Four further real conversations and 32 new queries compared six conditions. Positive-scene hits were 12/16 with legacy cues and the source gate, and 14/16 when the original route was retained and search hypotheses added before the gate. Confusable false returns stayed at 1/8 versus 1/8; mix-ups were not resolved.

2026-10-21 · The comparisons shared a frozen database and saved actual AI outputs. Browsing runs neither an AI nor the retrieval engine.

The new gated condition still failed expected retrieval outcomes.

It returned a wrong scene on 1 positive query, a false candidate on 1 confusable query, and no candidate on 1 positive query: 3 failed expectations in total. Compatibility with source text and features does not establish that the intended event was retrieved.

The graph ran. The selected top-one source did not change.

Independent diagnostics found the same selected scene for all 32 queries in all six conditions under normal execution, disabled traversal (hops=0), and disabled convergence (convergence=false). No contribution of the graph or convergence to the final top-one selection was observed on this sample. The retrieval increase is not evidence of graph efficacy or a speed improvement.

02 / SIX COMPARISON CONDITIONS

Separate retrieval leads from answer adoption.

What a search hypothesis means

Non-unknown cues can open retrieval even when negated or hypothetical. Uncertainty alone does not stop the whole search. Becoming a search candidate does not make a statement true or authorize its use in an answer.

Preserve the original route and order

Original-query retrieval stays available. Its candidates come first when additional routes are joined; scores are not summed across routes. The legacy condition remains in the comparison, including its failures.

Two diagnostic conditions

original_gated applies the strict gate to the original route. positive_union_gated joins the original route with positive cues before that gate. They separate restoring the fallback route from retaining negation, hypothetical cues and uncertainty as search hypotheses.

Keep the strict source gate

recovery_raw and recovery_gated compare the same recovery retrieval with and without the gate. Strict checks of source links, source text and feature compatibility stay unchanged. Passing the gate alone does not establish person, object or event identity.

03 / FROZEN FRESH INPUTS

Which sources were frozen, and when.

Lock selection before semantic reading

From 171 archived sessions, 91 were excluded using retained prior-use and exposure records. The first four of 14 eligible sessions in chronological order and the first eight whole messages in each were selected. After mechanical counting, selection was durably fixed before semantic reading of selected bodies. Counting required decoding the body strings. The public pre_body_selection_locked flag refers to this pre-semantic lock.

Retain the exclusion-metadata repair

The initial lock excluded 101 sessions because a not_needed status flag had been incorrectly classified as reviewed. The mechanical classification was repaired, reducing exclusions to 91, and the effective selection was fixed before semantic reading of selection-packet bodies. All failed-lock and before/after artifacts were retained. Source suitability and retrieval performance were not used to reselect inputs. Unused refers to retained local exposure records.

Index without query tuning

An actual AI authored 64 source-only features and retained review outputs. The retrieval inventory contained 58 concepts. The index was frozen before query authoring; all six conditions shared sources, queries, target labels and saved AI outputs.

Scope of role separation

Indexing, query and target authoring, cue generation and review used separate instructed roles. Cue generation was blind to sources, targets and retrieval results. The query author read the full permitted protocol and was not blind to algorithm design, but did not read the index, retrieval outcomes or known regression results. Roles were separated by instructions on a shared host, without access isolation or external blinding. Model identifiers were unavailable.

PRE-SCORE SEMANTIC REVIEW

Retain requirements rejected before scoring.

The first actual AI semantic review rejected 5 arbitrary literal requirements. The author removed only those requirements and retained 1 repair round. This occurred before any fresh-query retrieval or scoring. Cues, queries, target labels, the index and the source gate stayed unchanged.

06 / VERIFICATION AND LIMITS

Replay agreement and remaining limits.

  • Comparisons replayed the same saved actual AI outputs. Regenerating AI interpretations to test their stability was not evaluated.
  • An independent replay checked agreement on the saved inputs.
  • 58 of 58 synthetic engineering checks passed. They are distinct from both the fresh and known 32-query sets and are not pooled into retrieval rates.
  • Source-scene labels and indexing were checked internally. Public JSON alone does not permit independent auditing of source meaning, source identity or engine execution.
  • Event identity, Emotion and full Wake remain unverified. Natural everyday recall and biological brain reproduction were not evaluated. Storage and retrieval were not timed in this study.

07 / IMPLEMENTATION STATUS

Separate experimental integration from operational deployment.

The new policy code was merged only into the private experimental branch as an explicit opt-in addition. The legacy policy remains the default. Experimental-branch integration and results on this sample do not establish operational deployment or everyday performance. The policy has not been introduced into the operational memory database, and no automatic model caller or full Wake was added.

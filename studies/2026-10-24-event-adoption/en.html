Was retrieved source evidence
used correctly in answers?

Four archived conversations, all 95 retained messages, and 32 frozen new queries. Alongside source-scene selection, saved actual AI answers were assessed as correct supported answers, correct abstentions, partial supported answers, missed answers, or wrong or unsupported answers.

The five exclusive answer outcomes sum to 32 per row. Correct supported answers use 16 supported queries; correct abstentions use 16 corpus-unsupported queries. These denominators are not pooled into one score. False retrieval returns and unsupported answers are different measurements.

Complete supported answers in normal execution were 11/16 without a gate, 12/16 with event review alone, 6/16 with the strict source gate and 6/16 with both. The strict and combined conditions missed 10/16 and 10/16 supported answers, respectively.

An actual AI answer is saved once for each unique query/source combination, then referenced again by conditions selecting that combination. Condition lookups are neither fresh AI generations nor independent answer samples. Four successful contexts, retained failed attempts, total calls, primary assessment and final audits are reported separately.

The index was manually created and reviewed by an AI reading source material only. There were 344 literal witness occurrences, 284 unique native feature entries and 230 canonical concepts. These are distinct units, not automatic learning from repeated retrieval. Roles were separated by instructions on the same host.

Reader wrapper references were opaque, but the original-message trace retained internal source-case IDs and archive provenance. Reader inputs had no direct query-kind, expected-source, arm, rank, score or condition mapping fields. Source-case identities were therefore not fully anonymous. Roles were instruction-separated on a shared host, without technical isolation. Frozen inputs were retained as used, without a retrospective retry to remove this limitation. Original messages and internal IDs are absent from public JSON and pages.

Reader and assessor call counts denote orchestration role/context invocations, not authenticated provider request IDs or service-request counts. Underlying model identity and settings were not exposed and were unavailable. One primary semantic assessor performed grading; semantic inter-rater agreement was not measured. Count and privacy audits do not constitute additional semantic graders.

Failures and corrections before scoring

The first index review rejected two subject-attribution errors. A later native build stopped on duplicate feature registrations before creating outputs. All 344 original witnesses were retained while duplicates were normalized to 284 unique registrations and reviewed again. These corrections preceded code and question freeze.

The first event review rejected five requirement judgments that claimed more support than their quoted fragments supplied. One pre-score repair changed only those judgments' support states, evidence and reasons. The same reviewer, in a context separate from the author’s, rechecked the repair. Overall candidate verdicts, originals, questions, index and native retrieval candidates were unchanged. Original versions, rejection, helper failures and repaired artifacts remain retained.

09 / PROPOSED RESEARCH

Retain the next research steps as unexecuted proposals.

PROPOSED / NOT EXECUTED

The next research proposal is to test coverage of each query requirement and a reread grounded in source evidence. A subsequent proposal would record real new episodes in this order: prediction before the outcome → actual action and result → next choice → retention of the history. It would use actual records rather than fictional episodes. This sequence was not executed in the present study and would not certify biological brain reproduction.

PUBLIC SCOPE / LIMITS

Keep the verification boundaries explicit.

This is an assessment of frozen source material and saved actual AI answers on a small selected sample. It does not verify reproducibility through fresh AI generation, natural daily recall, biological brain reproduction, AGI, Emotion or full Wake. The privileged ceiling does not establish operational performance.

Public material contains the method, all-condition aggregates, opaque strata and audit digests. It excludes original text, query text, source IDs, feature vocabulary, SQLite and private execution code. Public files support aggregate consistency checks, but not source auditing or retrieval-engine reruns.

Code engineering checks: 71/71. Synthetic procedure checks: 41/41. These are separate from the 32 real-query study outcomes. Independent audit of the public export was verified.

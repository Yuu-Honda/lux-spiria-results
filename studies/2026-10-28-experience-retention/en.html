STUDY / EXPERIENCE RECORD USE / IMMEDIATE

Can an AI use its own execution record
on the next task?

An AI chose its own operations, saw actual execution outcomes and authored an external experience record. Fresh AI contexts received that record or no record, then faced the same held-out objectives. We compare artifact acceptance and optional additional schema inspection.

2026-10-28 · Immediate execution record. The ≥24-hour trial is uncollected; scheduling is not a result.

01 / QUESTION AND FINDING

Read correctness and additional inspection separately.

The causal comparison is no experience record → the same frozen experience record. Current tasks, opaque handles, source families, actual source digests and optional inspection opportunities are equal. No current schema/format answer is pre-supplied. The common probe instructions contain no direct legacy-preservation instruction; the experience-record template does ask for the applicability of earlier conditions, with content chosen by the AI. The intervention is access to the whole record; it does not isolate review from raw experience.

On the same six items, acceptance was 6/6 without the record → 6/6 with it. Additional schema inspections were 6 → 5.

Paired items show 0 corrected and 0 regressed. Six items are three held-out objectives in two formats, not six independent objectives.

Artifact acceptance was equal; this sample shows no accuracy gain.

Across the six matched items, there were 1 fewer optional structural inspections. Equal acceptance with no added execution errors, invalid actions or abstentions meets the bounded benefit criterion for access to the whole frozen record.

Zero additional inspections does not mean zero total source I/O. In both arms, the executor reads the assigned file to calculate the artifact. Speed, token and compute savings were not measured.

03 / FROZEN HELD-OUT PROBES

Compare record access on the same held-out tasks.

Three held-out immediate objectives are executed in two formats: six items per arm, each arm/item in a separate fresh context, 12 contexts total. All 12 initial policies freeze before any inspection output. Only requested inspections run, then the same item context chooses its action. All 12 actions freeze before execution outcomes; submitted initial actions remain locked.

Each item permits at most one inspection and one action, with no outcome-based retry, probe memory update or cross-item conversation. Fixed dispatch is no-record first on odd IDs and record first on even IDs; this does not establish provider/time counterbalancing. Each execution exposes only its assigned source alias.

07 / LIMITS

Read this as use of an external experience record.

This is a descriptive engineered-format task over three known public numeric files and an artificial tool interface. Original values were previously public; prior exposure or training cannot be excluded. Code compares actual artifacts against typed expected values rather than grading semantic understanding or psychological learning.

Roles share a host with read-allowlist instructions, not OS isolation. Provider identity, settings, requests, tokens and compute are unavailable and remain null. These results do not establish natural recall, continuous identity, a particular LRE/SHIORI mechanism, binary speed/graph effects, native ChatGPT, full Wake/Emotion, biological brains, AGI or consciousness.

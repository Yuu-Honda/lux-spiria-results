STUDY / RETRIEVAL HYPOTHESES2026-10-21

More relevant hits. Mix-ups remained. ↗

Four further conversations and 32 new queries compared six conditions preserving original-query retrieval while adding negation, hypothetical cues and uncertainty as search hypotheses. The strict source gate stayed unchanged. The recovery policy still returned one wrong positive scene, returned one confusable false candidate, and missed one positive query.

Traversal and convergence ran, but independent diagnostics found unchanged final selections when either was disabled, for all 32 queries in all six conditions. The retrieval increase does not demonstrate a graph contribution or a speed improvement.

02 / METHOD

The procedure comes before the result.

Freeze the inputs

Freeze the source, index, questions and target sources before retrieval. Do not tune cues or targets after seeing the result.

Show every condition

Publish every comparison condition in each study. Separate target hits, false returns and pre-search abstention; report path and speed effects only when actually measured.

PUBLIC SCOPE

What is public, what is retained.

Overview, results and history remain separately traceable.

This Results site

Publishes the frozen procedure, all condition aggregates, opaque per-query results, verification and limitations. These pages display stored results; they do not run SHIORI or an AI while you browse.

Private research bundle

Original conversations, question text, source IDs, feature terms, SQLite, experiment code and detailed logs remain private. Public aggregates can be recalculated; source auditing and engine reruns cannot be performed from public files alone.

LUX SPIRIA history

The originating questions, past observations, README and releases remain in the original project. New experiments do not rewrite past records.

Not evaluated in this study

The latest study assesses newly generated and frozen actual AI answers. Fresh 12 queries and eight selected known regressions remain separate. Natural daily recall, personality continuity, brain reproduction, AGI, Emotion and full LRE Wake remain unverified.
